## Design Context

### Users
Customers looking for an everyday treat, focusing on self-indulgence and daily snacking.

### Brand Personality
Playful, colorful, modern, and approachable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Soft & Natural (Light) - Warm beige, soft creams, natural lighting, while keeping the modern, playful vibe.

### Design Principles
1. **Joyful Everyday Treats**: The interface should feel like opening a fresh box of cookies—approachable, delightful, and casual.
2. **Soft but Playful Palette**: Use a light, soft natural base (creams, warm whites) paired with vibrant, colorful accents to bring the playful modern personality to life.
3. **Distinctive Typography**: Avoid standard corporate fonts; pair a characterful display font with a clean, highly readable modern sans.
4. **Organic & Fluid Shapes**: Move away from rigid, templated layouts (like strict neumorphism or boring cards). Use soft curves, organic overlaps, and asymmetry.
5. **Tactile Interactions**: Interactions should feel satisfying and snappy, with micro-animations that add a subtle moment of delight without slowing down the user.
